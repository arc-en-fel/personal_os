# ⚡ Reminders - 60 Second Quick Start

## 🎯 How to Add a Reminder (30 seconds)

### Step-by-Step with Pictures

```
STEP 1: Open the app
  └─ See the tabs at bottom: ⌂ ◷ ◌ % 🔔 ? ≡

STEP 2: Find Calendar
  └─ Look for calendar in navigation or go to home tab

STEP 3: Find an Event
  └─ Look at calendar for any event
  └─ Example: "Doctor Appointment" or "Meeting"

STEP 4: Tap the Event
  └─ Screen shows event details
  └─ See: Title, Date, Time, Location

STEP 5: Scroll Down
  └─ Find "Reminders" section with "+ Add" button

STEP 6: Tap "+ Add"
  └─ New screen appears: "Add Reminder"

STEP 7: Choose WHEN
  ┌─ At event time ○
  ├─ 5 minutes before ○
  ├─ 15 minutes before ○
  ├─ 30 minutes before ○
  ├─ 1 hour before ✓ ← TAP THIS
  └─ 1 day before ○

STEP 8: Choose HOW
  ┌─ 🔔 In-app alert ○
  ├─ 📱 Push notification ✓ ← TAP THIS
  ├─ 📧 Email ○
  └─ 💬 SMS ○

STEP 9: Tap "Add Reminder"
  └─ Screen goes back to event
  └─ See your reminder in list!

✅ DONE!
```

## 📱 How Push Notifications Work (30 seconds)

### The Timeline

```
You add reminder for 2:00 PM event, "1 hour before"
  ↓
System saves: "Send notification at 1:00 PM"
  ↓
Phone waits in background
  ↓
At 1:00 PM... 🔔
  ↓
Your phone shows notification on home screen:
┌────────────────────────────┐
│ Personal OS            1:00 │
│ Doctor's Appointment       │
│ Your event in 1 hour       │
└────────────────────────────┘
  ↓
You tap notification
  ↓
App opens with event details
  ↓
You're ready! ✅
```

### Key Points

- ✅ **Real-time**: Notification appears exactly when scheduled
- ✅ **Background**: Works even if app closed
- ✅ **Automatic**: No server needed (device handles it)
- ✅ **Sound + Vibration**: Alerts you with sound and shake

```
WHAT: Team meeting at 10 AM tomorrow
```

```
  ✓ No way you'll forget!
```

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need to keep app open for notifications?**
A: NO! Push notifications work even with app closed.

**Q: What if I'm sleeping when reminder comes?**
A: Sound + vibration will wake you (or go silent in quiet hours).

**Q: Can I have multiple reminders for one event?**
A: YES! Tap "+ Add" multiple times for different timings.

**Q: Will it actually notify me?**
A: YES! At exact time, your phone will show notification.

**Q: How accurate is the timing?**
A: Within 1 second - very accurate!

**Q: Can I change a reminder?**
A: Delete it and add new one with different settings.

**Q: What if I delete the event?**
A: Reminder is deleted too.

## 📞 Troubleshooting

**Reminder not showing?**
- Pull down to refresh on Reminders tab
- Make sure you actually tapped "Add Reminder" button
- Check Reminders tab exists (🔔 icon)

**Notification not arriving?**
- Make sure notification type is set to "📱 Push"
- Give app permission to send notifications (asked first time)
- Check your phone notification settings

**Can't find the Reminders tab?**
- Restart app
- Look at bottom bar: ⌂ ◷ ◌ % 🔔 ? ≡
- 🔔 is the 5th icon
